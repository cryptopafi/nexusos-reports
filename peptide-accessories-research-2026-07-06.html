❌ Hard Blockers — Read Before Everything Else

🚫 Bacteriostatic Water (BAC water) on Amazon
Amazon's Professional Healthcare (PHC) Seller Policy PDF explicitly names "Bacteriostatic water" as requiring PHC program membership. PHC is invite-only. FBA not available for PHC products.
Amazon all stores • Hard block
🚫 Hypodermic Needles + Insulin Injectors on Amazon
Same PHC Seller Policy PDF explicitly lists "Hypodermic needles and insulin injectors." Hard blocked for standard sellers. FBA not available.
Amazon all stores • Hard block
🚫 BAC Water in UAE
UAE authorities treat BAC water as a pharmaceutical excipient / prescription-channel item. Compounding pharmacies (CureCell) can sell; general e-commerce sellers cannot. Walk-up pharmacy purchase rarely possible without prescription.
Amazon.ae + all UAE channels
🚫 UAE Medical Device Registration (EDE)
Emirates Drug Establishment (replaced MOHAP Dec 29, 2025) requires full device registration per product. Official fees AED 16,100. Total realistic cost USD 15,000–50,000+ per device. Timeline: 6–12 months. Per-shipment import permits also required via a Local Authorized Representative.
All UAE channels • Class I–IV devices
🚫 OTC Pharmaceutical Products on eMAG Romania
Romanian Pharmacy Law 266/2008 + OM 444/2019. eMAG has published an explicit notification: OTC drug sellers must hold a pharmacy license. BAC water = pharmaceutical = blocked for standard marketplace sellers.
eMAG.ro • Pharmacy license required

Stream 2 — Best Practices 2026

Amazon.ae — FBA vs FBM

  • FBA available for non-Rx medical devices and general accessories. Minimum FBA fulfillment fee: 7.5 AED per unit (weight/size tiered). Source: sell.amazon.ae/pricing.
  • FBA NOT available for Rx/PHC products — Amazon will dispose of inventory at seller's expense.
  • Professional selling plan: Required for gated products; monthly fee currently waived on Amazon.ae (limited-time promotion).
  • Seller Flex (hybrid): Two-tier fee card from Aug 2025; AED 75 price threshold divides rates.
  • PPC costs in this niche: No UAE-specific data found. UNCERTAIN

eMAG — Fulfilment by eMAG (FBE)

  • FBE available in Romania only (not Hungary or Bulgaria). Single account sells to all three markets.
  • Free storage for 3 months on enrollment; additional 3 months free if 80,000 RON in first quarter.
  • Zero upfront costs — no monthly fee, no listing fee, no account fee. Pay only commission on sale.
  • Cross-border: Free translations into Romanian, Hungarian, and Bulgarian provided by eMAG.
  • Approval: 1–2 weeks. Choose up to 5 product categories at registration with sample product list.
  • Medical devices (CE-marked): Can be listed by non-pharmacy sellers with CE certificate + EU Declaration of Conformity. Full documentation list requires direct eMAG inquiry.

Listing Optimization

  • Amazon.ae: Arabic/English dual titles recommended; A+ content available for brand-registered sellers; review generation via Vine program (invite-based for new listings).
  • eMAG: EAN codes mandatory; images must meet eMAG spec; "Open Romania" badge available for Romanian-manufactured products — increases visibility.

Reseller responsibilities under EU MDR (for CE-marked products):
  1. Verify CE marking is present and valid — check EUDAMED database
  2. Verify manufacturer's EU Declaration of Conformity
  3. Ensure correct labeling in EU languages + UDI (Unique Device Identifier) where required
  4. Report serious incidents to national authority (NAMMDR in Romania)

Resellers do not need their own Notified Body certification if sourcing already CE-marked products. Sources: business.gov.nl; rimsys.io CE marking guide; anm.ro/en/dispozitive-medicale/marcaj-ce

Romania ANMDMR / NAMMDR

NAMMDR confirms: CE-marked devices may be placed on the Romanian market and put into service freely. EU MDR 2017/745 applies in full — no Romanian-specific additional layer beyond EU requirements for standard devices.

Medicinal products (including BAC water) require NAMMDR marketing authorization — not a viable path for a marketplace seller.

Source: anm.ro/en/dispozitive-medicale/marcaj-ce (official Romanian competent authority).

Stream 4 — Platform Policy

Amazon.ae

  • "Medical Devices & Accessories" is a restricted category on Amazon.ae requiring approval. Source: sell.amazon.ae/sell.
  • OTC medical devices can be listed by standard sellers with compliance documentation (all product images, instructions, manufacturer device listing, classification evidence).
  • PHC-blocked products: Amazon's PHC Seller Policy PDF explicitly names Bacteriostatic water AND Hypodermic needles + insulin injectors as requiring PHC participation. PHC is invite-only.
  • PHC program on Amazon.ae: The PHC program is documented for the US only. Whether an equivalent exists on Amazon.ae is UNCERTAIN. Requires direct Seller Central UAE inquiry.
  • Sharps containers: Not named in PHC blocked list. May be listable as OTC device. UNCERTAIN — ASIN-level check required.
  • Ungating standard products: Requires manufacturer invoice + optionally Letter of Authorization. Professional selling plan required.

eMAG.ro

  • No pharmacy license required for most non-pharmaceutical health products.
  • OTC pharmaceutical products (incl. BAC water): Pharmacy Law 266/2008 applies. eMAG has published a specific notification stating OTC drug sellers must comply with pharmacy licensing. BAC water = pharmaceutical = BLOCKED for standard sellers.
  • CE-marked medical devices: Can be sold by non-pharmacy sellers. eMAG's surgical mask notice shows minimum docs required: CE certificate + EU Declaration of Conformity. Full list for other medical devices requires direct eMAG inquiry.
  • Documentation requirement specifics: UNCERTAIN — requires direct eMAG marketplace team inquiry.
