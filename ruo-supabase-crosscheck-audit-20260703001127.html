Methodology

A full paginated snapshot of all 24 tables was pulled live, bounded at 2026-06-30, and reconciled by a deterministic engine across eight check groups: referential integrity (orphans), stock-ledger vs on-hand balance, order↔movement, restock↔movement, financial line arithmetic, payments/receivables, duplicate/reversal detection, and stored-vs-recomputed checksum drift. Findings were then classified by evidence — separating documented cleanup artifacts and pre-ledger opening balances from genuine anomalies — to avoid false alarms.
