6.1 GLP-1 Churn — The #1 LTV Risk

This is the single most important risk to model.

HorizonClinical adherence (real-world)Retention driver
Month 1~90–95%Novelty, early weight loss visible
Month 3~70–75%First plateau, escalation side effects (nausea)
Month 6~55–65%Cost fatigue; 14.1% avg weight loss by M6 for completers
Month 12~50–80% (wide range)Insurance disruption, weight plateau, perceived "done"

Why churn is so high — HealthVerity shows 20–50% discontinuation in Y1

  1. Side effects at dose escalation (nausea, vomiting) — especially months 1–3
  2. Cost pressure — $119–299/mo is a significant recurring expense without insurance
  3. Weight plateau — patients expect linear loss; a plateau without coaching triggers dropout
  4. Perceived completion — some stop at goal weight, unaware regain is near-certain without maintenance
  5. Insurance disruption — coverage changes, prior authorization lapses

Coaching and behavioral programs are the proven churn mitigation: WW data shows members who engage lose 29% more weight AND have longer subscription life.

7 · EU/UK Unit Economics Context

Publicly available EU/UK benchmarks are sparse (all major operators are private). Key structural differences:

  • Drug COGS is higher — branded Wegovy/Mounjaro at NHS/private list prices significantly compresses operator margins vs. US compounded model.
  • CAC is lower in some EU markets due to lower Meta CPM and less saturated competition.
  • Regulatory ad restrictions (EU Dir 2001/83/EC Art 88 on Rx drug advertising) require service-positioning, not drug-promotion, reducing conversion rates.
  • Estimated EU blended CAC: €80–150 (internal estimate for new entrants; no public benchmark). LTV lower given higher COGS.
  • UK Numan pricing strategy (£46.20/mo entry, 12-mo commitment) suggests sub-£200 average revenue per subscriber for first months, escalating with dose increases.

8.4 Stated Assumptions — Flag Every One

1. Drug legality assumption: Compounded semaglutide/tirzepatide available via a Netherlands compounding pharmacy partner to Romanian patients. UNCONFIRMED. Counsel gate required before pricing. If only branded (Ozempic/Wegovy) is permissible, drug COGS increases 3–5x, entry price minimum rises to €350+/mo.

2. Digital ad accessibility: Meta/Google must accept a compliant service-positioning claim (not drug-promotion). Currently uncertain for prescription GLP-1. LegitScript certification or equivalent may be required. If ads are blocked, CAC rises significantly.

3. Influencer CAC: Base case assumes a Romanian influencer channel reduces effective CAC to €150–200 in Year 1. If unavailable, CAC reverts to paid-media range (€300–400).

4. GLP-1 churn is the #1 risk: If coaching/behavioral program is not active from Month 1, Month-3 retention may fall to 55–60% (below the No-Go gate). Budget for dietitian engagement from launch.

5. Netherlands pharmacy lead time: A compounding partner requires 4–8 weeks for onboarding + legal supplier agreements. On the critical path.

6. Platform COGS: Estimates assume a white-label telehealth stack at ~€5–15/subscriber/month platform fee. If building in-house, platform COGS drops but development timeline extends 6–12 months.

9 · Key Strategic Conclusions

01Price the entry GLP-1 at €149/mo with drug included. Matches the cheapest US provider (Henry Meds) in USD; affordable enough for Romanian adoption while leaving margin if compounded. Avoid going below €129/mo — margin floor is tight.

02Bundle coaching from Day 1, not as upsell. Clinical data shows coaching reduces dropout by 29%+. ROI on dietitian time is strongly positive at any payback period under 18 months.

03GLP-1 churn is the #1 financial risk. A 1pp improvement in monthly churn (e.g. 4%→3%) increases 12-month LTV by ~15%. Over 1,000 subscribers, that's €100K+ annually. Invest in plateau intervention, proactive messaging, and side-effect support before scaling marketing.

04Launch ED alongside GLP-1 from Month 1. ED margins are structurally better (65–78% vs. 50–60% for GLP-1). Cross-selling GLP-1 subs to ED within 90 days is the fastest route to blended ARPU improvement. Combo bundle (€179 vs €198 separate) is a proven DTC mechanic.

05Target LTV:CAC of 3:1 by Month 18. Sustainable floor for scaling. Below 3:1, marketing spend cannot compound. Hims reached 6:1 at 2.5M subscribers; plan for 3:1 as the Year 1 target.

06EU operators are more expensive than US compounded but have structural moats. Branded-only EU pricing (£164–339/mo UK) means a compounded entry at €149 is a genuine price advantage — IF compounding is legally accessible. If not, parity with EU incumbents; differentiation must come from service quality, language, and local trust.

07Dubai premium pricing supports a €300–400/mo all-inclusive tier. The GCC expat audience does not price-compare with the Romanian market. If entering Dubai alongside Romania, maintain separate pricing tiers (€299 Romania Standard ≈ AED 1,195 Dubai ≈ competitive vs Zone.Health AED 1,295).
