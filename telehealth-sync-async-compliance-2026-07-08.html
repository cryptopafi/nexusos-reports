Telehealth Sync vs Async Consultation Compliance 2026

EPR15/20 D3 Research DELPHI PRO 2026-07-08
No jurisdiction imposes a blanket mandatory synchronous requirement — but the picture is highly fragmented. The Netherlands offers the most permissive regime; UAE/Dubai is the most restrictive with Standard Seven mandating real-time audio-video.

Across five target jurisdictions (Romania, Greece, Netherlands, UAE/Dubai, Saudi Arabia), this report maps synchronous vs asynchronous consultation modality requirements for ED (sildenafil/tadalafil) and GLP-1 (semaglutide/tirzepatide) telehealth prescribing.

2Per-Zone Analysis
🇷🇴 Romania ASYNC-OK MEDIUM Confidence

Binding framework: OUG 196/2020 (enabling legislation) + HG 1133/2022 (implementation norms). Art. 9 of HG 1133/2022 specifies scheduling and patient file access requirements but does not differentiate sync vs async, or first vs follow-up consultations.

First consultation: No explicit sync requirement found. Six permitted service types (teleconsultație, teleexpertiță, teleasistență, teleradiologie, telepatologie, telemonitoring) are defined without a sync/async constraint. Absence of prohibition ≠ explicit permission — COUNSEL-GATED GAP-RO-01.

GLP-1 risk: Colegiul Medicilor din România (CMR) may have binding guild standards requiring in-person assessment for GLP-1 initiation — not answered by legislative framework [GAP-RO-02].

Pending: 2024 ministerial ordinance draft referenced but not yet enacted. May impose sync requirement when enacted [GAP-RO-04].

Sources: OUG 196/2020 · HG 1133/2022 · UNICEF Ghid Telemedicina 2022 · Jurnalul.ro 2024
🇬🇷 Greece UNCLEAR LOW-MEDIUM Confidence

Critical gap: Law 5129/2024 amended the Code of Medical Ethics to permit examination "either in person or remotely through a telemedicine system" — permissive text that encompasses async. However, the implementing ministerial decree defining operational conditions has not been issued as of early 2026. Until issued, the operational framework is legally undefined.

First consultation: Statute text is permissive enough to encompass async. The implementing decree (when issued) may impose sync conditions. Any Greek-based telehealth platform operates with a material legal gap [GAP-GR-01].

EOPYY reimbursement: Separate pathway modality rules for ED and GLP-1 may apply via the national health insurer — not found in available sources [GAP-GR-02].

Sources: Law 5129/2024 · Law 4931/2022 · Law 3984/2011 Art. 66 · Lawgroup.gr 2024 · ICLG Greece 2025
🇳🇱 Netherlands ASYNC-OK HIGH Confidence

Binding rule: "Regeling Online Medicijnen Voorschrijven" (Decree on Online Medicine Prescribing), Staatscourant No. 10521, effective 12 April 2023. Explicitly permits online prescribing WITHOUT physical consultation if: (a) physical examination is clinically unnecessary; (b) prescriber accesses current medication history.

No video mandate: The April 2023 Policy Rule does not require synchronous (video) consultation. The clinical judgment standard replaced the previous COVID-era video tolerance policy. KNMG professional guidelines reference video but are non-binding.

ED: Async-first viable — experienced clinician can reasonably determine physical exam unnecessary for established ED with screened cardiovascular risk.

GLP-1: Structured questionnaire + lab results upload likely satisfies the "medication history access" condition. No explicit sync mandate, but IGJ enforcement interpretation for GLP-1 initiation should be confirmed [GAP-NL-02].

MVP Recommendation: Netherlands is the only jurisdiction where async-first for ED first prescriptions can launch with reasonable legal confidence (pending COUNSEL-GATED confirmations in GAP-NL-01 through GAP-NL-04).
Sources: Staatscourant 10521 (Apr 2023) · KNMG Video Consultation Guidelines · Lexology Apr 2023 · BioTalk/TwoBirds Analysis · CMS.law Netherlands
🇦🇪 UAE / Dubai (DHA ST-14) SYNC-MANDATORY HIGH Confidence

Binding standard: DHA/HRS/HPSD/ST-14 Version 4, effective 26 November 2025 (revision date 26/09/2030), published under CIR-2025-00000212. Standard Seven = TELECONSULTATION: SYNCHRONOUS — real-time audio-video interaction governs all direct patient-provider consultations resulting in clinical decisions and prescriptions.

Standard Eight (Telediagnosis) permits async for diagnostic interpretation only (radiology, pathology). Prescribing consultations fall exclusively under Standard Seven.

Exclusions (binding): Emergency cases; narcotic/controlled/semi-controlled medications; in-person consultation platforms. Neither sildenafil nor semaglutide is controlled under Federal Decree-Law No. 30/2021 — exclusion clause does not apply. Standard Seven still governs.

§6.5.5 ambiguity: "Telehealth limited to advisory and follow-up care" language raises whether first-prescription new patient consultations are within permitted scope — COUNSEL-GATED [GAP-UAE-01].

Additional mandatory requirements: Care pathways registered with DHA prior to service; patient consent via secure electronic means; documented encounters; valid DHA telehealth license; servers in UAE (ISO 27001); multi-factor authentication.

Sources: DHA ST-14 V4 (Nov 2025) · CIR-2025-00000212 · Kayrouzandassociates.com Jun 2026 · DHA Telehealth Policy 2021
🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia ASYNC+VIDEO LOW Confidence

Primary text inaccessible: MOH Telehealth Regulations and NHIC Governing Rules retrieved in binary PDF format — full text not extractable. Findings rely on Al Tamimi & Company secondary analysis.

Al Tamimi analysis: "Teleconsultations must involve a video consultation (cannot be solely audio) but need not be synchronous." This creates a novel "video-async" category — patient records video or physician reviews async video — but rests on secondary analysis of inaccessible primary text. COUNSEL-GATED [GAP-SA-01, GAP-SA-06].

Practice evidence: The official Sehha app uses live synchronous video in practice. Operational practice does not establish legal requirement, but suggests de facto sync norm.

Drug risks: Tirzepatide SFDA registration status in KSA should be confirmed [GAP-SA-03]. SFDA dispensing chain compliance required for telehealth prescriptions [GAP-SA-02]. SCFHS registration for foreign physicians serving KSA patients [GAP-SA-05].

Sources: Al Tamimi & Company analysis · PMC/Cureus Sehha App studies · Hammad & Al-Mehdar 2024 · CMARIX blog MOH/SFDA

Rome I overlay (consumer contracts): If a physician's platform targets Romanian consumers, Romanian consumer protection law may apply to the contractual relationship — not to the medical act itself.

Rome II overlay (tort): In liability scenarios, the law of the country where damage occurs (likely Romania) may govern — relevant for malpractice exposure under both Dutch and Greek-based platform models.

GLP-1 Medications (semaglutide, tirzepatide) — Heightened Risks

1. BMI/metabolic assessment: GLP-1 initiation requires documented BMI ≥ 30 kg/m² (or ≥ 27 with comorbidity) per EMA/FDA labeling. Async models must include verified weight/height documentation — lab upload pathway may suffice.

2. Diabetes diagnosis: For T2D indication, HbA1c and diagnosis history documentation required. Structured async intake with lab upload is plausible.

3. Ongoing monitoring: GLP-1 therapy requires periodic weight, adverse event, and renal function monitoring. Pure async model risks non-compliance — hybrid model (async initiation + scheduled sync follow-up) may be required.

4. Supply constraints: Semaglutide (Ozempic/Wegovy) remains on EMA shortage lists as of mid-2026 — confirm availability in patient's dispensing country before initiating.

5. Off-label use: Prescribing for off-label purposes via async telehealth carries elevated professional liability in all jurisdictions.
