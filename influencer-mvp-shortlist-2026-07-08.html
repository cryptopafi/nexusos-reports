5 Key Observations From the Model

  1. Single mid-tier RO creator (200K, health niche) can yield 120-240 paying patients in 1-2 campaigns — sufficient to hit the €30K/month target without macro-level reach.
  2. The 30-50 validation bar is achievable with one micro or mid-tier creator in a single campaign, estimated cost €3-8K total. Payback within month 1.
  3. Engagement rate beats follower count for medical conversion. 100K at 4% engagement outperforms 500K at 0.5%.
  4. ED converts 1.5-2x faster than GLP-1 per follower touched (shorter decision cycle, anonymous online channel preferred). For MVP CAC/LTV validation: ED-first gives fastest signal.
  5. Commerce proof is the #1 qualifying signal: creators already selling subscriptions/programs have demonstrated their audience pays in the health category — directly predictive of telehealth conversion.
