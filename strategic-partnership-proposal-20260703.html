Strategic Partnership Structuring Proposal

Prepared: 2026-07-03

Confidential: for the incoming partner, the founding investor, and the operating team

Financial basis: the frozen 2026-06-30 audit. Every figure in this document is audited and will not move.

This proposal invites the incoming partner into the Company (research use only peptides operation), as a strategic partner rather than a capital provider. The company has a working engine: audited gross margin of 74.18 percent, EUR 48,663.78 of stock at retail value, and a May that proved real demand. What it lacks is what the incoming partner brings: recurring customer inflow from Ibiza and Dubai, and the Amazon.ae (Amazon United Arab Emirates) relationships to launch a peptides accessories line that the peptide products themselves can never touch. We have structured the offer so the incoming partner's capital enters on exactly the same terms as the founding investor's, and equity reflects the strategic delivery only the incoming partner can make.

1. Executive Summary

  • →The loan, unchanged: EUR 25,000 as a shareholder owner loan, matching the founding investor's audited exposure baseline of EUR 24,757.33, on paper identical to the founding investor's, ranking pari passu (equal ranking) with the founding investor. Signed before any wire.
  • →The equity, reformulated: 20.00 percent participation locks at wire. The remaining 13.33 percent vests on two measurable milestones, taking the incoming partner to a full 33.33 percent.
  • →The milestones: M1 is EUR 7,500 of incoming-partner-tagged delivered revenue within 90 days (vests 6.67 percent). M2 is the Amazon.ae accessories line live within 120 days (vests 6.66 percent).
  • →Paper first: conversion mechanics, seniority, and governance are signed on day one. No handshake equity. If the milestones do not land, the EUR 25,000 stands as a protected loan and the 20.00 percent stays.
  • →Funds: 100 percent to operations on a published allocation, never to repay the founding investor, tracked in a separate owner-loan ledger. A standby second tranche of EUR 15,000 to 25,000 is pre-agreed for the moment a milestone fires.
  • →Your choice: if you prefer to prove the channels before committing capital, a 90-day paid pilot is on the table with the same measurement rails (Section 8).

4. The Ask, Reformulated

The original idea was EUR 25,000 for one third, paper later. Stress-tested by a five-lens advisory council, it failed in both directions: it underpriced the incoming partner (money now against paper later, seniority undefined) and it underpriced the company (the full third granted before either channel delivers). Here is the structure that fixes both.

The instrument. EUR 25,000 shareholder owner loan, single wire, on paper identical to the founding investor's, ranking pari passu with the founding investor's EUR 24,757.33 baseline. That baseline is itself audited: USD 14,929.91 of unrecovered historical funding (EUR 12,907.33 at the audit FX rate) plus EUR 11,850.00 of owner-loan salary and fuel rows, with a USD 2,330.00 capital return already deducted. Everything is signed before the wire, together with the conversion side letter. The incoming partner is never an unsecured afterthought.

M1 is calibrated modestly: less than the company delivered in May alone, spread across a full quarter. Attribution is mechanical, not judged. The incoming partner receives unique referral codes per channel, tagged at order entry, live and tested before the wire.

If milestones do not land. The EUR 25,000 remains a loan on founding-investor-equivalent terms. Participation stays at 20.00 percent. Landing one milestone vests its slice. There is no scenario in which the incoming partner loses the loan protection.

The company's obligations run the other way too. Supplier lead time is about 37 days. If the company lets a designated fast mover go out of stock, milestone clocks pause day for day. The incoming partner cannot be timed out by our supply chain.

The company stops granting its last third for promises, because the premium above capital parity is paid for delivery. This is mutual de-risking, not a loyalty test. If the incoming partner prefers to prove first and commit after, Section 8 is an equally honorable door.

6. The Accessories Opportunity: Amazon.ae

Research use only peptides can never be listed on Amazon. Accessories can. Repeat-purchase consumables on Amazon.ae ride scalable Gulf fulfillment rails and carry none of the peptide products' listing restrictions. This is effectively a second business inside the deal, and the incoming partner's relationships are its key.

Our proposal: treat accessories as a distinct line with enhanced economics for the incoming partner, structured as a joint venture (JV) whose split is agreed in the side letter before launch. One honest caveat: Amazon.ae operations will likely require a United Arab Emirates (UAE) entity, and written legal advice may place that entity outside this cap table. We will not blur that line; keeping the research use only peptide entity clean protects the equity being earned. M2 stands regardless of which entity hosts the listings.

7. Governance and Alignment

The founding investor is a party, not a bystander. The incoming partner's 33.33 percent is created by pro rata dilution of all existing holders, so no single owner funds it alone, and the founding investor countersigns the full package: loan ranking, dilution, and earn-in. Nothing in this deal is bilateral.

Three equal owners need deadlock rules. The shareholders agreement will include reserved matters (owner repayments, new owner debt, dilution, channel exclusivity, major contracts), a pre-named tie-breaker mechanism with mediation before any buy-sell, and leaver plus clawback provisions so vested equity tracks continued delivery. Channels are attributed, not exclusive: the incoming partner keeps attribution economics on tagged customers, the company keeps the right to serve any market. No one owns a chokepoint.

Treasury discipline. Funds sit in a separate owner-loan ledger with custody reporting. Full transparency: a single capital return of USD 2,330.00 to the founding investor occurred before the audit cutoff; it is disclosed in the frozen 2026-06-30 audit and already deducted from the founding investor's EUR 24,757.33 baseline. From signing onward there are no owner repayments to anyone, the founding investor included, until the owners jointly approve a repayment plan. The incoming partner's money funds operations, and that is verifiable rather than promised.

Supply reciprocity. The company commits to keep designated fast movers in stock, and milestone clocks pause during any company-side stockout, as defined in Section 4.

8. The Lighter Alternative: A 90-Day Pilot

If the incoming partner would rather price the channels with real data before buying into the cap table, we welcome that. Structure: a 90-day paid commission and distribution pilot on the same referral-code rails, commission on incoming-partner-tagged delivered revenue at a rate agreed on the call, no capital in, no equity, weekly dashboard. If the incoming partner converts to the partnership within 30 days after the pilot, tagged pilot revenue counts toward M1. Choosing this door is not read as doubt; it lets the numbers speak first.

9. Open Items Before the Final Cap Table

The audit exceptions, on the table before signing rather than after:

  1. Ownership terms are unsigned. This proposal exists to fix that.
  2. Owner-fund custody proof is pending physical, bank, and crypto confirmation for the Ibiza Logistics Lead and Bucharest Operations Lead treasuries.
  3. The exact Bucharest Operations Lead treasury figure depends on the treatment of a RON 75 (Romanian leu) item.
  4. A customer's EUR 110.99 credit remains a customer liability until delivery.
  5. Purchase order SO-04 (about USD 1,300 / EUR 1,123.89) is in transit and excluded from cutoff stock.
  6. Overhead absorption into inventory begins only under the policy effective 2026-07-01.
  7. A written legal opinion on research use only status for Ibiza and Dubai consumer inflow, and on the Amazon.ae accessories structure, is required before scaled consumer marketing. This is professional counsel work, and we will not assert it informally.

11. How This Proposal Was Stress-Tested: Council Appendix

Five independent advisory lenses reviewed the original ask:

Contrarian: do not sell the last third for cash before delivery; sign seniority and conversion now. Adopted.
First Principles: demand, not capital, is the binding constraint; cash is the bond, equity pays for delivery. Adopted as the core logic.
Expansionist: the channels and Amazon.ae are the real assets; pre-commit a standby tranche so a win can be funded. Adopted, with revenue projections deliberately excluded.
Outsider: from the incoming partner's chair, money now and paper later fails; sign day one, disclose all capital flows, put legal questions in writing. Adopted.
Executor: three documents, two countable milestones, attribution before the wire, weekly dashboard. Adopted as the execution spine.
Council verdict: proceed with the earn-in structure above, keep the pilot open as an honorable alternative, require the founding investor's countersignature on everything, and obtain a written legal opinion before scaling consumer channels.
